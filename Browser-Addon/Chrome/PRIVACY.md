# Privacy Policy — TextTrace

_Last updated: 2026-08-23_

TextTrace ("the extension") is a browser extension that searches for text and
regular expressions across files in a local folder that you explicitly choose.
This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with your data.

## Summary

**TextTrace does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any user data.**
Everything the extension does happens locally, inside your own browser, on your
own device.

## What data the extension accesses

To perform a search, TextTrace reads the contents of files inside a folder that
**you** select through the browser's folder‑picker dialog (the File System
Access API). It only reads folders you actively grant access to, and only while
you are using it to search.

The extension uses this access solely to:

- scan the files in the folder you chose,
- find text or regular‑expression matches, and
- display those matches to you on screen.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does **not** send your files, file names, paths, search terms, or results
  to any server, third party, or external service.
- It does **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry.
- It does **not** collect personal information, authentication information,
  financial information, health information, location, browsing history, or user
  activity.
- It does **not** store your data anywhere outside the current session. Results
  exist only in the open tab and are discarded when you close it.

## Local exports

If you choose to export your search results (CSV, JSON, or HTML), the file is
generated locally in your browser and saved directly to your device through the
normal download mechanism. These exported files are created on your computer and
are never uploaded anywhere by the extension.

## Permissions

TextTrace requests only the minimum access needed to function: the ability to
read a folder you pick, and to open its own interface. It does not request access
to your browsing history, other websites, or background network activity.

## Data sharing

None. Because no data is ever collected or transmitted, there is nothing to
share, sell, or disclose to third parties.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's behavior ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this
document will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
